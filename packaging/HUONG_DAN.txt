PHAN MEM QUAN LY CAN BO - HUONG DAN CAI DAT (ban 4.x, file .exe)
=================================================================

Yeu cau: Windows 10 hoac Windows 11 ban 64-bit. KHONG can cai Python hay thu vien nao.

CAI DAT / NANG CAP
  1. Giai nen file zip nay ra mot thu muc bat ky.
  2. Bam dup CAI_DAT.bat.
     - Phan mem duoc cai vao %LOCALAPPDATA%\QuanLyCanBo, tao bieu tuong
       "Quan Ly Can Bo" tren Desktop va Start Menu, roi tu mo phan mem.
     - Neu may da cai ban cu (ban chay bang Python), du lieu cu (canbo.db,
       backup, file dinh kem) duoc GIU NGUYEN va dung tiep ngay.
  3. Lan dau: dang nhap admin / admin@123, phan mem se yeu cau doi mat khau.

CHAY KHONG CAN CAI DAT (dung thu)
  Mo thu muc QuanLyCanBo va bam dup QuanLyCanBo.exe. Du lieu se nam ngay
  trong thu muc do.

GIOI HAN MAY DUOC CAI (tuy chon)
  - Tren tung may, bam dup XEM_MA_MAY.bat de lay ma may.
  - Ghi cac ma may vao allowed_machines.example.txt, doi ten thanh
    allowed_machines.txt, dat canh CAI_DAT.bat roi moi cai dat.

QUEN MAT KHAU ADMIN
  Mo cua so lenh trong thu muc cai dat va chay:
      QuanLyCanBo.exe --reset-admin
  (dat lai admin / admin@123, phai doi mat khau khi dang nhap)

CO CAU TO CHUC
  Don vi cua can bo chon theo: Cong an tinh / thanh pho -> Phong hoac
  Cong an xa, phuong -> Doi / To (tu nhap). Mac dinh: Cong an tinh Hung Yen.
  Quan tri vien sua danh muc phong cho dung co cau thuc te tai muc
  "Cau hinh module" > "Co cau to chuc".
